01 / ABOUT

About me

I'm a robotics engineer who works where robots actually have to run: in production, outdoors, around people and machinery — not just in simulation. My current work is field robotics: system setup and validation, structured autonomy testing, sensor profiling, and debugging live robots with logs, ROS bags, and the tools that show what really happened.

That field work sits on an embedded foundation: firmware in C and C++, PCB design and bring-up, control systems, and sensor fusion. Whether it's a humanoid arm, a mobile robot, or a fleet of autonomous units, I enjoy bringing hardware, code, and mechanics together into systems that keep working after deployment.

Build it, test it in the field, fix what breaks, ship it again.
